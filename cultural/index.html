Alaap

The Symphony of Indian Music Throughout the Ages

Event Brochure

View Brochure

Alaap is a two-day-long singing event celebrating the rich heritage of Indian music throughout the ages. From the captivating melodies of Bollywood, to the soulful thumri's of Ustaads and Pandits, we've got it all. The event brings together talented and aspiring musicians from Kolkata and other cities to perform before a live audience and a panel of distinguished judges and professional artists

Popular among students and professors alike for its tantalizing performances and cultural elegance, Alaap attracts performers of a variety of genres every year, showcasing their talent and creativity while competing for a cumulative prize pool of ₹7,000.

🎤

Soulful Performances

Experience captivating renditions of a variety of Indian musical compositions by emerging and established artists from across India.

🏆

Competition Highlights

Over 60 participants compete across categories, judged by experts in Indian music, for a total prize pool of ₹7,000.

🎶

Cultural Harmony

A celebration that bridges tradition and talent — fostering appreciation for the timeless beauty of Indian music.

Between The Lines

The Art of Written Expression

Between the Lines is an online creative writing contest that challenges writers to uncover the layers of their own thoughts. Open to participants across India, the competition features two categories — below 18 (school students) and above 18. Participants can write in English or Bengali and are free to choose the form of writing — poetry, prose, or essay.

Bringing together voices from every corner of the country, Between the Lines creates a stage for budding writers to earn recognition and make their mark.

Thru Da Lens

Capturing Moments, Creating Stories

Event Brochure

View Brochure

Thru Da Lens is a pan-India online photography contest celebrating the art of visual storytelling. This competition is open to all photography enthusiasts across the nation, offering an exciting experience regardless of age, with a special invitation extended to students from schools and colleges to showcase their unique perspectives. Participants are challenged to capture compelling and poignant moments that align with a central theme.

With a prize pool of ₹6,000, this event provides a vibrant digital platform for emerging photographers to showcase their creativity, gain national visibility, and connect with a community of creative minds.
